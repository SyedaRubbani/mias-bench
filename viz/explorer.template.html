Turn Order Collapse

A mixed-initiative system specifies which agent speaks first. The LLM serving engine decides, on the basis of KV-cache residency, batch composition and preemption. This shows how much of the design survives.

Every round at a glance

Inside one round

Each lane is one agent, in designed speaking order. The pale bar is prefill, the solid bar is decoding, and the notch where they meet is the first token — the moment that agent starts speaking. Click any row above to inspect it.

Simulated with a reference model of vLLM's block-paged KV allocation, prefix caching, continuous batching and LIFO preemption. Timing constants are placeholders, not hardware measurements — see the repository's limitations section.
